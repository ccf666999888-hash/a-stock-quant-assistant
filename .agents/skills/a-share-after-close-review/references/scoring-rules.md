# Scoring Rules

## Sector score

- Relative strength versus all-A: 20
- Breadth: 15
- 60-minute technical state: 20
- Amount continuity: 10
- Three-day and five-day relative persistence: 10
- AKShare provider fund flow: 15 when available
- Limit-up ecology: 10 when available

When a supplement is unavailable, rescale observable sector components instead of assigning a zero penalty. Show the missing field explicitly.

## Stock score

- Sector quality: 20
- Relative strength versus sector: 15
- Daily technical structure: 15
- Volume-price and close quality: 15
- Stage and overheating controls: 10
- Individual 60-minute state: 10
- AKShare provider fund-flow confirmation: 15

Deduct for long upper shadows, weak closes, huge-volume stalls, RSI/KDJ overheating, excessive MA bias, excessive five-day return, and risk technical states.

## Selection gates

- Exclude ST, delisting-risk names, recent listings, daily amount below CNY 100 million, and severe missing data.
- Priority candidates require a positive sector state, positive individual 60-minute state, no severe risk, and a non-retreat market.
- Priority candidates require positive Eastmoney main-flow ratio and positive combined extra-large plus large net flow. Tonghuashun total-flow data can support sector scoring and conditional stock status but cannot substitute for this large-order confirmation.
- Conditional candidates may include strong-slowdown or neutral sectors only with superior stock relative strength.
- Exclude sealed limit-up stocks from the ordinary pool. Put qualified non-one-word first boards into a separate relay pool.
- Downgrade to retreat when the broken-board rate is at least 40% with at least 10 limit-down stocks, when limit-down stocks outnumber limit-up stocks, or when all-A breadth falls below 35%.
- A first-board relay candidate requires score at least 70, positive sector state, fund-flow confirmation, non-one-word tradeability, and no retreat market. Prefer earlier sealing, no more than two opens, adequate seal amount relative to float market value, and 3%-20% turnover.
- Return no candidates when none qualify. Do not loosen thresholds to reach a target count.
- Default maximum: five priority and five conditional names.
